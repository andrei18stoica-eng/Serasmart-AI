# Listening to your community — Serasmart AI

## Input
Texts the user pastes or attaches (reviews, comments, support tickets, forum threads), or public pages found with web search.

## Analysis
1. Sort every mention into: praise, complaint, question, idea, comparison with a competitor.
2. Group them into themes; for each theme give how often it appears, a typical quote (short, without the person's name), and whether it is getting better or worse if dates are known.
3. Spot people who could become fans or partners (they help others, they post often) — by public username only.

## Deliver
- Top 5 themes with what to do about each (fix, explain, build, thank).
- Three answers ready to post for the most common questions or complaints.
- A short summary for the team (5 lines).

Never collect private data about individuals; work with what is public and with the user's own data.
