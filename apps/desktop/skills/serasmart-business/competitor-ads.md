# Watching competitors' ads — Serasmart AI

## Input
Competitor names or links, screenshots of their ads, or their public ad library pages (for example the Meta Ad Library), plus the user's own offer.

## Analysis
For each ad or page: the promise (headline), the problem it targets, the proof used (numbers, reviews), the offer (price, discount, trial), the call to action, the format (image, video, carousel).
Then find patterns: which promises repeat, which audiences they target, what nobody is saying.

## Deliver
- A comparison table: competitor × promise × offer × call to action.
- Three gaps the user can own.
- Five ad ideas for the user (headline, one line of text, visual idea), original — never copy a competitor's text or creative.
