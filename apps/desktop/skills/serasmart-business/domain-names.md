# Domain and brand names — Serasmart AI

## Input
What the business does, for whom, the tone (serious, friendly, premium), languages it must work in, preferred endings (.ro, .com, .eu).

## Generate
20 ideas in four groups: descriptive, invented words, combinations of two words, metaphors. Rules: 6–14 letters, easy to spell after hearing it once, no hyphens or digits, no meaning that is bad in Romanian or English.

## Check each finalist (top 5)
- Pronunciation and spelling in the languages that matter.
- Similar names already used by companies in the same field (search the web) — avoid confusion with existing brands.
- Trademark risk: tell the user to check EUIPO (TMview) and OSIM for Romania before buying.
- Availability: tell the user to check the domain at a registrar (for .ro: rotld.ro) — Serasmart AI cannot buy or reserve domains.

Give the top 5 with a one-line reason each.
