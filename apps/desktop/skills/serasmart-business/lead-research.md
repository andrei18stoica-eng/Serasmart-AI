# Finding clients — Serasmart AI

## Define the ideal client first
Ask (or infer from the user's site): industry, company size, region, who decides, what problem the product solves, typical budget. Write it as a short "Ideal client" card.

## Research
- Use web search when available; only public information (company sites, public registers, press, job ads, public social profiles of companies).
- For each company: name, website, size, why it fits (a concrete signal: new location, hiring, a problem visible on their site), who might decide (role, not private contact data), and one personalised opening line.
- Score each 1–5 for fit and explain the score in one line.

## Deliver
A table sorted by score, plus the 3 best with a short note on how to approach them.

## Rules
- No private personal data (private phone numbers, home addresses, personal emails). Business contact details only when the company publishes them.
- Respect GDPR: cold emails in the EU go to business addresses, with a clear way to opt out.
