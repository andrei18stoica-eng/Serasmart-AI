# Market data from public sources — Serasmart AI

## Sources to prefer
- National statistics offices (in Romania: INS — insse.ro), Eurostat, the World Bank, the IMF, central banks (BNR for exchange rates and interest rates), the ECB.
- Company filings and annual reports, stock exchange pages (in Romania: BVB), public registers (ONRC data, Ministry of Finance company balance sheets).
- Industry associations and serious press.

## Steps
1. Agree on the question (market size, growth, prices, competitors, a company's results).
2. Collect numbers with web search when available; write down the source and the date of every number.
3. Normalise: same currency (state the exchange rate and its date), same period, real vs nominal values clearly marked.
4. Build a short brief: key numbers table, a chart description or an Excel file if asked, 3–5 conclusions, the risks in the data.

## Rules
- Never present an estimate as an official figure; label estimates and explain how you got them.
- This is information, not investment advice.
