# Outreach to a prospect — Serasmart AI

## Input
Who the prospect is (company, role, one fact about them), what the user sells, and one result the user achieved for a similar client.

## Sequence
1. **Email 1 (≤ 90 words):** a line about them (the fact), the problem you think they have, one proof (result with a number), one simple question as the call to action ("Would a 15-minute call next week help?"). Subject: 3–6 words, no clickbait.
2. **Follow-up after 3 days (≤ 50 words):** a new useful piece (a tip, a short case), same question.
3. **Follow-up after 7 days (≤ 40 words):** polite close ("Should I stop writing?").
4. **LinkedIn note (≤ 280 characters).**

## Rules
- No false familiarity, no fake "Re:", no invented results.
- Offer an easy way to say no; in the EU write only to business addresses.
- Give two subject lines to test.
