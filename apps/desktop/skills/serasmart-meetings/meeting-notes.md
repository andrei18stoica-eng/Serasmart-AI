# From meeting to actions — Serasmart AI

## Input
A transcript (from Zoom, Teams, Meet or a recorder), a summary, or the user's own notes. Ask who attended if it is not clear.

## Output
1. **Summary in 5 lines:** what was discussed and agreed.
2. **Decisions** (numbered), each with who decided.
3. **Action items** as a table: task — owner — deadline — status. A task without an owner or a date is marked "to assign".
4. **Open questions and risks.**
5. **Follow-up email** to all participants, ready to send.

## Insights (when asked)
Who spoke how much, questions left without answers, topics that keep coming back across meetings, tone (agreement, tension) — described neutrally, without judging people.

Keep names as written in the transcript; do not guess who said something if the speaker is not marked.
