# Preparing a meeting — Serasmart AI

## Input
Who meets, why, how long, and anything known (previous emails, notes, the other company's website).

## Deliver (one page)
1. **Goal:** the one decision or result that makes the meeting worth it.
2. **Agenda with minutes:** for a 30-minute meeting usually 5 / 15 / 7 / 3 (context, main topic, decisions, next steps).
3. **What to know:** about the people (role, what they care about — only professional, public information) and about their company (recent news, size, products).
4. **Questions to ask** (5–7, open), and **objections to expect** with short answers.
5. **Materials to bring or share.**
6. **The follow-up email draft**, ready to complete after the meeting.
