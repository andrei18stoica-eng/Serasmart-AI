# Team updates — Serasmart AI

## Digest of a channel or thread
From the messages the user pastes (or a connected app): what was decided, what is blocked and who must act, questions still open, useful links. Five to ten bullets; quote nobody at length.

## Announcement
- Title that says the news ("New expense rules from 1 November").
- What changes, for whom, from when, what people must do — in this order.
- Under 150 words; a link or attachment for details; who to ask.

## Weekly status
**Done** (3–5 results, not activities) · **Next week** (3 priorities) · **Blocked / need help** · one number that shows progress.

Use the tone of the team (formal or casual) and the language they write in.
