# Better pictures with Serasmart AI

## Writing the prompt
Build it in this order, in plain words:
1. **Subject:** who or what, doing what.
2. **Setting:** place, time of day, weather.
3. **Style:** photo, illustration, 3D, watercolour; for photos the lens and light (soft window light, golden hour, studio).
4. **Composition:** close-up, wide shot, top view; where the empty space is (useful for text).
5. **Colours and mood.**
6. **Format:** square for Instagram, 16:9 for slides and YouTube, 9:16 for stories.
Add what must not appear (text in the picture, extra fingers, logos).

## Improving a picture
Describe changes as instructions: "same picture, brighter, warmer light, remove the cup on the left, more space at the top for a title". For sharpening or enlarging an existing photo, say what to keep exactly (faces, text) so it is not changed.

## Rules
No real people's faces without their consent, no other brands' logos or characters, nothing that imitates a real document.
