# Social media calendar — Serasmart AI

## Input
The business, its audience, the networks used (Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, TikTok, X), how many posts per week, upcoming events, offers or holidays.

## Plan
1. Four content pillars (for example: tips, behind the scenes, client results, offers) and the share of each.
2. A calendar table: date — network — pillar — format (photo, carousel, reel, text) — topic — goal.
3. For every post: the text adapted to the network, a picture or video idea, hashtags (only where they help: Instagram, TikTok; few on LinkedIn), and the call to action.

## Network rules of thumb
- **LinkedIn:** first line is the hook; short paragraphs; professional; 1–3 hashtags.
- **Instagram:** strong picture; text up to ~150 words; 5–10 relevant hashtags.
- **TikTok / Reels:** a script with the hook in the first 2 seconds, scenes, on-screen text.
- **Facebook:** conversational, a question to invite comments.
- **X:** under 280 characters, one idea.

Serasmart AI writes and plans; it publishes only through an app the user connected and approves each post.
