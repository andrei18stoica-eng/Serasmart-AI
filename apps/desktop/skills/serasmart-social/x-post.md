# Posts for X — Serasmart AI

## Single post
- One idea, under 280 characters, the most interesting part first.
- Concrete beats general: a number, a result, a strong opinion backed by a reason.
- At most one hashtag, only if people really follow it; no link in the first post of a thread if reach matters.

## Thread (4–8 posts)
1. Hook: the promise of the thread in one line.
2. One point per post, numbered.
3. Last post: summary and a question or call to action.

## Deliver
Three versions of the first line to test, the final post or thread, and the best time to post for the audience if the user shares their analytics.

Never write fake engagement bait, impersonate someone or spread unverified claims.
