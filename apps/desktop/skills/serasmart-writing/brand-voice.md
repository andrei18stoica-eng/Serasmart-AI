# Writing in your voice — Serasmart AI

## Learn the voice
Ask for 3–5 real texts the user or the company wrote (emails, posts, website pages). From them, write a short **Voice profile**:
- Typical sentence length and paragraph length.
- Formal or informal; how readers are addressed ("tu", "dumneavoastră", "you").
- Words and phrases that come back often; words never used.
- How texts open and how they end (question, call to action, signature).
- Use of emoji, exclamation marks, lists, numbers.
Show the profile and ask the user to correct it.

## Write
- Follow the profile in every new text; when in doubt, choose the simpler wording.
- Keep the facts the user gives exactly as given.
- Offer two short variants when the tone could go two ways (more formal / more friendly).

## Check before sending
Read the text against the profile: would a colleague recognise it as theirs? Remove anything that sounds like generic marketing.
