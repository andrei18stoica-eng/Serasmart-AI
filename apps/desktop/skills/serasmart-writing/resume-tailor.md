# Tailoring a CV to a job — Serasmart AI

## Input
The user's current CV (attached or pasted) and the job ad. Ask for both if one is missing.

## Steps
1. From the ad, list the 6–10 requirements that matter most (skills, tools, experience, languages).
2. Match each requirement to proof in the CV. Mark the gaps honestly — never invent experience, titles, dates or degrees.
3. Rewrite:
   - **Profile (3 lines):** role, years of experience, two strengths that match the ad.
   - **Experience:** newest first; 3–5 bullets per job, each starting with a verb and, where possible, a number ("Cut delivery time by 20%").
   - Use the ad's own words for skills the person really has (helps automatic CV filters).
   - Move the most relevant experience and skills to the top; shorten unrelated parts.
4. Keep it to 1–2 pages; no photo, age or marital status unless the user asks.
5. Offer a short cover letter (150–200 words) that connects the person's best proof to the company's need.

Deliver as a Word file when the user wants to send it.
